# Research Auto

Supervise the authorized assignment within the user's agreed goal and current stage. Keep the user's selected supervisor model and effort. Research may be broad and technically detailed; user-facing reports should restore context and select the few findings that matter. Brief reporting does not reduce proof, verification or completion obligations.

## Read for the current stage

- [Supervision](references/supervision.md): planning, route selection, scientific value and course correction.
- [Delegation and models](references/delegation-and-models.md): work ownership, complete packages, independent checks and cost; read before dispatch.
- [Unattended research](references/unattended-research.md): only for explicitly continuous, overnight or unattended work.
- [Explain Project](../explain-project/SKILL.md): the common reporting method, including context recovery and responses to annotations.

Reuse unchanged references across cycles. A focused question stays bounded; an authorized substantial program may have several complementary modules.

## Work through cycles

1. Recover the final goal, current evidence, confirmed decisions, active owners, allowed scope, resources and stopping condition from the existing records.
2. Plan an ambitious but bounded advance within the selected stage. Separate the overall goal from modules and worker questions; leave proof routes and later stages open where exploration is authorized. A new direction needs user selection; an already approved agenda need not be selected again.
3. Dispatch nonduplicative work, inspect its actual result, independently check consequential claims, and integrate evidence with its assumptions and limits.
4. After each substantive round or material return, check correctness and fit to the original goal. Continue valuable supporting routes with a purpose and return condition; redirect unproductive detours. A first result is a checkpoint, not automatic completion of an open-ended program. Adopting a different goal, metric, information model or user-gated stage requires the relevant author decision.
5. Update material changes once in the existing records. Report through the common method above rather than reproducing worker packets. Incorporate confirmed feedback into the next cycle and replace superseded requirements. Follow the unattended continue/wait/hand-back rules when applicable.

Research authorization does not itself authorize a new chat, schedule, external message, manuscript rewrite or unlimited spending. On an explicit pause, interrupt workers, save the actual state and stop.
