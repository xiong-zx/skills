# Composition and conservative revision

## Establish the passage or document

Determine the reader, question/decision, language, the point each passage must establish, its evidence/reasoning, limiting conditions and established terminology. Use the author's accepted prose as a voice sample; otherwise use restrained academic language. A missing argument or result stays visible instead of becoming confident filler.

For several paragraphs, sketch their distinct jobs privately unless an outline is requested. For a note or memo, use the smallest useful structure: question, relevant setting, current findings, support and unresolved issues may suffice. Longer drafts need coordinated section responsibilities, not a compulsory paper template, paragraph count or section quota. Name headings for actual content, introduce context before dependent reasoning and place qualifications beside the claims they limit.

For each important statement, distinguish proved, cited, experimentally supported, inferred, conjectural and open. Attach its support or explicit status; a polished proposed result is not an established result. Conjectures, proof sketches, provisional sections and open decisions can remain in notes when labeled honestly.

## Draft for understanding

- Give each paragraph a clear step in the argument. Lead with the result, question, distinction or context needed next and make nearby paragraphs' relations intelligible, without prescribing one opening or sequence.
- Make the main subject, action and logical relation easy to find. Prefer a direct verb when abstract noun chains hide the action. Longer sentences are fine when their clauses serve one inference; supply only background needed for the next step.
- Explain technical objects by their purpose and effect, not by ceremonial prose or merely repeating a displayed definition. Define terms near first substantive use and reuse them; do not rotate synonyms for mathematical objects, methods, metrics or experimental groups.
- State theory claims with their assumptions/regime and distinguish the guarantee from interpretation. For empirical comparisons, identify comparator, metric, setting and uncertainty. Keep causal and novelty claims within actual support, with precise source locators for imported results.
- In experimental analysis, first give data-anchored observations: direction, magnitude, comparator and regime. Then explain their higher-level scientific/practical meaning. When several results share a conclusion, analyse them at data level before one shared synthesis. Mechanism interpretations must match the evidence; distinguish a plausible account from an isolated causal effect and retain contrary results.
- Match the author's first-person usage, formality and pace. Write connected exposition rather than slogans, directives or decorative headings. Preserve deliberate variation; do not manufacture it.

## Reusable author language conventions

- Use "distribution" rather than "law" for a probability distribution in authored prose, while preserving exact source titles, quotations and named results.
- Prefer direct setup sentences. Separate incidental properties instead of interrupting the point with embedded qualifiers. Use semicolons sparingly when they clarify related clauses or complex lists; keep sentences complete and connected rather than producing abrupt fragments. Avoid comma-heavy clause chains and nonessential appositives; integrate essential qualifications or give them a connected sentence.
- Attach actions to the correct objects. For example, deploy a decision, sample scenarios from its distribution, solve a subproblem and minimize an objective when those are the model's operations; do not call a decision both deployed and sampled when the observations are scenarios. Preserve the actual mathematical contract rather than imposing the example's vocabulary on another model.
- Introduce examples in a separate sentence rather than inserting "for example" midway through a mathematical statement.

## Check once and revise conservatively

Read at a natural pace: can the intended reader find the point, why to believe it and where it applies? When multi-paragraph flow is unclear, privately identify paragraph roles and repair the broken connection. Remove setup/repetition that delays the point; leave effective sentences alone.

Use the existing draft and author decisions as the baseline. Change the smallest coherent passage that fixes the requested problem, then recheck its context and support. For authorized structural consolidation, move, cut or merge complete passages before incidental paraphrasing; rewrite a paragraph as a unit only when redundancy, complexity or a missing connection requires it, so the redline exposes substantive changes.

Preserve numbers, units, notation, assumptions, theorem status, citations, evidence levels, experiment conditions, limitations and open questions. Never invent sources/results, repair a proof through wording or smooth over a scientific gap. For composition from Chinese materials or ordinary note feedback, recover the intended meaning and write idiomatic prose without inventing logical relations or claims. A dedicated post-draft paper language/AI-prose audit belongs to `review-manuscript`.

## Maintain author requirements without multiplying rules

When the author explicitly supplies a reusable general writing requirement, consolidate it here or in the directly relevant shared reference as part of the same task, within the authorized file scope, and report the update. Paper-specific requirements belong to `doc-paper`, review criteria to `review-manuscript`, and project-specific mathematical claims/conditions to the project. Do not infer global rule changes from a local correction, duplicate existing guidance or ignore an explicit no-file-change instruction.
