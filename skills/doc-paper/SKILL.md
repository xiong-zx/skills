# Publication Paper

First read [writing-research](../writing-research/SKILL.md) and its stage-relevant references. This is the direct shared baseline; no manuscript intermediary is required. Apply the paper-specific requirements below. Incomplete drafts are allowed, but unresolved support cannot masquerade as an established contribution or submission-ready result.

## Read for the current work

- For paper framing, contribution claims or readiness, read [paper contract and claims](references/paper-contract-and-claims.md).
- For whole-paper organization, section integration or empirical results, read [structure and evidence](references/structure-and-evidence.md).
- For an outline, title, Abstract or Introduction, also read [argument and Introduction](references/argument-and-introduction.md).
- For mathematical statements, proofs, notation or pseudocode, read [mathematical exposition](references/mathematical-exposition.md). It distinguishes requirements, defaults and flexible advice.
- For a theorem-driven paper, also read [theory-paper completeness](references/theory-paper.md). Formal mathematical development uses `doc-math`; do not accumulate another exposition template here.

Load only relevant references; specialized paper conventions override the general writing baseline. Verify current official venue requirements when venue preparation is in scope. Drafting can begin without a chosen venue, but final venue readiness cannot.

## Work and hand off

Identify the reader, authorized evidence, existing edits and requested mode. For a local revision, preserve effective prose and repair the smallest coherent passage. For a new draft or an authorized structural rewrite, recover the exact claims, dependencies, evidence and author decisions from the research materials. Use a short outline to choose a reading order before drafting. Reuse effective parts of the existing draft while preserving its scientific scope and the author's decisions.

Keep the question, leading contribution, claim strength, conditions and evidence consistent across title, Abstract, Introduction, results and closing sections. Follow the author's workflow for review and handoff.

During drafting, mark missing support explicitly. Before claiming readiness, resolve it or remove the unsupported established contribution, then verify sources, rendered output and venue compliance. Use `review-manuscript` for post-draft paper audits or language polish, and the relevant specialized audit for a disputed proof. Report exact blockers and actual verification status; preparing/checking files does not authorize upload or submission.
